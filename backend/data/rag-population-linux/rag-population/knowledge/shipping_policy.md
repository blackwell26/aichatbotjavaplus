# Shipping policy

Standard shipping normally arrives within three to seven business days after dispatch. Express shipping normally arrives within one to two business days. Delivery estimates exclude weekends and statutory holidays.

When an order ships, the customer receives an email containing the carrier and tracking number. Tracking may take up to 24 hours to become active. Customers can also find tracking information under Order History.

An order can be cancelled before the warehouse begins processing it. After processing begins, the customer must wait for delivery and use the return process. Address changes are not guaranteed after an order is submitted.

If tracking shows delivered but the parcel is missing, the customer should check safe-drop locations and ask household members. If it is still missing after 24 hours, customer support can open a carrier investigation.
