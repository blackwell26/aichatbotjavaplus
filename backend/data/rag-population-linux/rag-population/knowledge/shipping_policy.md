# Shipping policy

## Shipping options

Standard shipping normally arrives within three to seven business days after
dispatch. Express shipping normally arrives within one to two business days.
Delivery estimates exclude weekends and statutory holidays.

## Tracking

When an order ships, the customer receives an email containing the carrier and
tracking number. Tracking may take up to 24 hours to become active.

Customers can also find tracking information under Order History.

## Cancellation and address changes

An order can be cancelled before the warehouse begins processing it.

After processing begins, the customer must wait for delivery and use the return
process. Address changes are not guaranteed after an order is submitted.

## Missing delivered packages

If tracking shows delivered but the parcel is missing, the customer should:

1. Check safe-drop locations.
2. Ask household members.
3. Wait 24 hours in case the carrier updated the scan early.

If the parcel is still missing after 24 hours, customer support can open a
carrier investigation.
