# Refund status

## Refund timing

Refunds are issued to the original payment method after the warehouse completes
inspection of the returned item.

## Processing time

Inspection usually takes two business days. Banks may take an additional five
to ten business days to post the refund.

## What to tell the customer

If a refund is delayed, confirm:

- The return was received by the warehouse
- The payment method is still active
- The customer is checking the correct bank or wallet account

## Escalation

If the refund window has passed and the customer still has no posted refund,
escalate to support with the order number and return label reference.
