# Returns and exchanges

## Return eligibility

Customers may return most unused products within 30 calendar days after delivery.
Items must be in original condition and include all packaging and accessories.

## How to start a return

Sign in to your account, open Order History, select the order, and choose
Return items. Select a reason and download the prepaid return label.

Guest customers should contact customer support with:

- Order number
- Purchase email address
- Brief description of the issue

## Refund timing

Refunds are sent to the original payment method after the warehouse inspects
the returned item. Inspection usually takes two business days.

Banks may take an additional five to ten business days to post the refund.

## Non-returnable items

Final-sale items, personalized products, opened software, and digital downloads
cannot be returned unless defective.

## Damaged or incorrect items

Report damaged or incorrect items within seven days of delivery so support can
approve a replacement or refund path.
