# Returns and exchanges

Customers may return most unused products within 30 calendar days after delivery. The item must be in its original condition and include its packaging and accessories.

To start a return, sign in, open Order History, select the order, and choose **Return items**. Select a reason and download the prepaid return label. Guest customers should contact customer support with their order number and purchasing email address.

Refunds are sent to the original payment method after the warehouse inspects the returned item. Inspection normally takes two business days. Banks may take an additional five to ten business days to post the refund.

Final-sale items, personalized products, opened software, and digital downloads cannot be returned unless defective. Damaged or incorrect items should be reported within seven days of delivery.
