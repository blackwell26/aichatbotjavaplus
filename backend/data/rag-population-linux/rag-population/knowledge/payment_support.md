# Payment support

The store accepts major credit cards and supported digital wallets. Payment authorization occurs when the order is placed. The final charge is captured when items are prepared for shipment.

A declined payment may result from an incorrect billing address, expired card, insufficient funds, or a bank security control. Customers should verify their details and contact their bank before trying again.

For security, customer-service agents must never request or record a full card number, card security code, account password, or one-time verification code.

When an order contains multiple shipments, charges may appear separately. The total of all captured charges will not exceed the confirmed order total unless the customer later approves an order change.
