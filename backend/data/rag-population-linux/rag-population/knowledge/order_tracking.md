# Order tracking

## Where to find tracking

Customers can view tracking information from Order History or from the shipping
confirmation email.

## Tracking delays

Tracking may take up to 24 hours to become active after a shipment label is
created.

## Delivered but missing

If tracking shows delivered but the parcel cannot be found, advise the customer
to check:

- Doorstep or mailroom drop-off locations
- Household members
- Building reception or parcel lockers

If it is still missing after 24 hours, open a carrier investigation.

## Partial shipments

Some orders ship in multiple packages. Each package may have its own tracking
number and delivery scan.
